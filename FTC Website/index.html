01 / A little about us
AJ
JRCurious by design

Small team. Big ideas. Always building.

AimBot JR is a FIRST Tech Challenge team from St. Paul's College in Hong Kong, bringing ideas off the page and onto the field. We design, build, and program a robot together, learning from every test, every match, and every teammate along the way.

Our goal is bigger than a good score: grow our skills, show up for our community, and make something we're proud to put our name on.
